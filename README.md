An entry for the [make.fun](https://make.fun) bounty **"The Funniest Office Joke, Judged Head to Head"** — one short line about office life, judged head to head by an AI judge.

## The joke

> We had a meeting about having too many meetings; it ran over, so we scheduled a follow-up to discuss why it ran over.

A meeting about too many meetings that runs over — the problem reproducing itself is the punchline, and it lands last.

## How it works

`main.ts` writes the single-line joke (117 characters, well under the 200-character gate) to `/output/entry.txt`:

```sh
node main.ts
```
